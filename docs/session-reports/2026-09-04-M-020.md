## Outcome

- `collectVisibleSurface(sceneRoot)` traverses only explicitly supplied render/display nodes and
  returns either `ui_state: dialogue` or `ui_state: unknown`.

  renderable ancestors.

  ancestors fail safely to `unknown`.
